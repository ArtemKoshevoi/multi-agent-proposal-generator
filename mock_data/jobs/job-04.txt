React Native Developer Needed — SMS Review Automation App (iOS + Android + Web Dashboard)
Summary
I'm building QwikRev — a B2B SaaS app for local home service businesses (plumbers, electricians, cleaners, landscapers, HVAC technicians, etc.).
 
The concept is simple: after finishing a job, a field worker opens the app, enters the customer's name and phone number, and the app automatically sends them an SMS asking for a Google review.
 
Smart routing handles the rest:
• 1–3 star rating → customer is taken to a private feedback form (message goes to the business owner only — never public)
• 4–5 star rating → customer is taken directly to the business's Google Review page
• Link not clicked within 24 hours → one automated reminder SMS is sent automatically
 
The app is free to download on iOS and Android. All subscription payments are handled on our website via Stripe — no in-app purchases.
